# Changelog

<!--
The one file to edit to ship an update. The release workflow reads the newest section, the one at the top:

- Its heading is "## major.minor.patch", and " - YYYY-MM-DD" may follow it. Minor and patch run 0 to 99.
  The version names the build and the tag (v1.0.0), and Google Play's version code is
  major * 10000 + minor * 100 + patch, so 1.0.0 is 10000 and 1.2.3 is 10203.
- Under it goes one "- " line per note, each on a single line and written for players. They become the
  GitHub release notes and Play's "What's new", which holds 500 characters in all.
- Play never accepts a version code twice, even for a rebuild or a build that only went to a test
  track. So every new upload, however small, gets a new section with at least the patch number bumped.
- The game shows this version in Settings and About, and its tests check this file with the same rules
  as the workflow, so a section the workflow would refuse fails locally first.

To ship: add a new section at the top, push, then Actions > Release Android > Run workflow.
-->

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-04

- Twenty-five chapters, each with its own rule, colour and pattern: gates, portals, springs, magnets, glass and more.
- Difficulty only ever climbs, from a brand new opening five.
- Stuck? Solve 14 of a chapter's 20 and the next one opens, or open any level with a key or a short video.
- Every win says something new, and makes a card to share, save or watch as a reel.
- A shop with every level, marble sets and colour themes, each free for 6 hours with a video, and volume sliders.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-02

- Panels open all the way on every phone, and a swipe down closes them.
- Swipe the level grid sideways to turn the page.
- A quicker start: two marbles from level 4 and a second colour from level 9.
- The icon shows whole on your home screen, and the game opens with a little spin.
- The Supporter pack is a tap away from the title screen and the hint prompt.
- The credits fit on the screen, and About's help link is now called Help.

## 1.0.0

- Swipe to tilt the board and every marble rolls at once. Roll each one into the hole of its own colour.
- A short guide on the first level shows you the ropes, and How to play in Settings brings it back.
- Light and dark themes, or let your phone pick.
- Settings are a tap away on every screen, with sound, music and vibration you can switch off.
