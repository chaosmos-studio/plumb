# Changelog

<!--
The one file to edit to ship an update. The release workflow reads the newest section, the one at the top:

- Its heading is "## major.minor.patch", and " - YYYY-MM-DD" may follow it. Minor and patch run 0 to 99.
  The version names the build and the tag (v1.0.0), and Google Play's version code is
  major * 10000 + minor * 100 + patch, so 1.0.0 is 10000 and 1.2.3 is 10203.
- Under it goes one "- " line per note, each on a single line and written for players. They become the
  GitHub release notes and Play's "What's new", which holds 500 characters in all.
- Play never accepts a version code twice, even for a rebuild or a build that only went to a test
  track. So every new upload, however small, gets a new section with at least the patch number bumped.
- The game shows this version in Settings and About, and its tests check this file with the same rules
  as the workflow, so a section the workflow would refuse fails locally first.

To ship: add a new section at the top, push, then Actions > Release Android > Run workflow.
-->

## 1.4.8

- One short video now gives you three hints, not one.
- Stuck on a level for a while? The hint button gives you a little nudge.
- No video ready? Hints, locked levels and pack tries are simply free, with no quick-game ads in between.
- The banner for our other games matches your light or dark look.
- The intro now hands over to the home screen quicker.

## 1.4.7

- No more blank screen at launch. Plumb opens on the Chaosmos mark, and the intro now settles gently into Home.
- The seesaw on the home screen stays in its place when the game starts, instead of slipping behind the Play button.

## 1.4.6

- Plumb now opens on a short Chaosmos intro, told with its own marbles. Tap to skip it.
- It opens straight into the intro, with no icon screen first.

## 1.4.5

- The banner stays at the bottom of every screen as you move around, from the moment the game opens, the win sheet included.

## 1.4.4

- The banner at the bottom of the screen shows every time again, under the level grid, the board and the win sheet.

## 1.4.3

- Your stars show on the title right after your progress is restored.

## 1.4.2

- Fixed a rare crash when closing the game.
- The banner now sits further from the navigation bar, so it's harder to tap by mistake.
- Pressing back on the title screen now keeps the "Leaving already?" question open.

## 1.4.1 - 2026-10-24

- Share your daily puzzle time as a picture, with that day's board and a code to scan.
- Turn a tablet or an open foldable sideways and the game turns with it.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-10-22

- A new daily puzzle opens after the first chapter: race the clock, keep a streak and catch up on missed days.
- Sign in with Play Games for leaderboards, achievements and progress that follows you to a new phone.
- More room between the buttons and the bottom banner, so a tap never lands on it by mistake.
- A hint can come with a short video, or with a quick game of one of our other puzzles as before.
- Now and then an ad may come between levels again, never during one.

## 1.3.1 - 2026-10-18

- The back button on a solved level takes you to the level grid.
